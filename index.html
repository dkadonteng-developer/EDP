1. Harbour Receipt

Raw material (clinker, gypsum, slag) is received at the harbour and moved to the storage shed.

2. Storage Shed

Bulk stockpile storage before material is drawn down to the feed hoppers.

3. Feed Hoppers

Clinker, gypsum and slag hoppers proportion each material via individual feeders.

4. Weighfeeder (Feed Table)

Meters the proportioned blend onto the feed conveyor at a controlled rate.

5. Grinding Mill

Centre-discharge ball mill grinds the blended material into fine powder.

6. Bucket Elevator

Lifts the ground material up to the separator for classification.

7. Separator

Process fan and air slide classify the material — fines pass on, coarse particles are rejected.

Rejects are recirculated back to the Mill (step 5) via screw conveyor for re-grinding.

8. Cyclone

Separates the fine product from the air stream before it's carried onward to the silo.

9. Transport to Silo

Product reaches the silo by either of two routes:

Pneumatic Transport pipe, carried by the flux pump
Mechanical Bucket elevator, direct from mill to silo

10. Silo

Finished cement is stored in the silo ahead of packing.

11. Bucket Elevator

Lifts product out of the silo up to the vibrating screen for the packing line.

12. Vibrating Screen & Hopper

Screens and stages product from the silo, ready for packing.

13. Screw Conveyor → Rotary Packer

Conveys cement to the rotary packer, which doses it into bags.

14. Dispatch

Packed cement is discharged and loaded out for delivery.
