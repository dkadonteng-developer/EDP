Source Parts Sign In

Sign in with your admin account to search suppliers and shortlist parts for procurement.

This uses the same admin accounts as the rest of InfoPort, but access here is restricted to Procurement specifically — being a Stores or general admin does not grant access to this page.

Search live supplier listings for any spare part by name, model number, or spec — results are ranked cheapest-first, like a flight search, so procurement can compare OEM and aftermarket sources at a glance before raising a purchase order. Current stock levels for every part are also visible below, so you can check what's already on hand before sourcing more.

Searching as Ghana surfaces more locally-relevant listings, but no search API can guarantee a specific seller ships to Ghana — always confirm shipping destination and cost with the supplier before ordering. If Ghana results look thin for a part, try United States, United Kingdom, Nigeria, or China from the dropdown for a wider pool.
