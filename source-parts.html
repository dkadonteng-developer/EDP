Source Parts Sign In

Sign in with your admin account to search suppliers and shortlist parts for procurement.

This uses the same admin accounts as the rest of InfoPort, but access here is restricted to Procurement specifically — being a Stores or general admin does not grant access to this page.

Search live supplier listings for any spare part by name, model number, or spec — results are ranked cheapest-first, like a flight search, so procurement can compare OEM and aftermarket sources at a glance before raising a purchase order. Current stock levels for every part are also visible here, so you can check what's already on hand before sourcing more.

About these results & prices (GHS rates by ExchangeRate-API)

Google Shopping has no Ghana-specific catalog, so results are always drawn from another country's listings — UAE and India are worth trying since they're common import routes for parts into Ghana; US/UK/Germany tend to have deeper OEM catalogs. GHS conversions update daily and are a guide, not the exact amount you'll be charged. No search API can guarantee a specific seller ships to Ghana — always confirm shipping destination and cost with the supplier directly before ordering.
